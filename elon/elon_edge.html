Elon Tweet Edge

Does the weather-market edge carry over to “how many times will Elon post?” markets? Short answer: some real information, but small and fragile. Shadow it, don't trade it.

GO-to-shadow, not live
Jargon in 5 lines
  • Sigma = how spread out a forecast is.
  • ROI = profit ÷ money staked.
  • p-value = chance of seeing a result this good by luck alone (lower is better).
  • Slippage = paying more than the mid price to actually get filled (1c = one cent per share).
  • Overround = bucket prices adding to more than $1.

1What this is

Every week Polymarket lists about 26 buckets (e.g. 200–224 posts) on how many times Elon Musk will post on X. Each bucket is a YES/NO share that pays $1 if the final count lands in it, and the winner is decided by Polymarket's own post tracker.

We buy YES on cheap buckets when our model says the chance is at least 5 percentage points higher than the price (and price is 50c or less), $10 a trade. The idea: the market sometimes prices the spread of outcomes too narrowly, so far-out buckets are too cheap.

2Why it can work

The marketPolymarket weekly (and 48h) “how many times will Elon post?” markets, split into count buckets such as 200–224, 225–249.
Settles onPolymarket's own post tracker (xtracker). Matched the winning bucket in 210 of 212 events since Dec 2025.
Where the edge comes fromOur model's forecast spread (sigma) is better matched to reality than the market's early in the week. At the start the outcome lands 1.25 SDs from the market mean and 35% of outcomes fall in the market's outer 20%. Cheap upper-tail buckets are under-priced.
When it's strongestAround 50% elapsed (+156%, p=0.03). 5–15c buckets are best (+81%, p=0.03). At window start it is gone (+15%, p=0.45) because bucket prices sum to about $1.30.
Why usCheap to run: the model and shadow logger are already built. Settlement is clean (6,125 of 6,132 markets resolved without issue).
RisksEdge is lumpy (top 5 events ≈ 95% of PnL), vanishes at 1c slippage, does not replicate on sibling markets, pros and bots are present, capacity is tens of dollars a week, tracker late imports are rising.
VerdictGO-to-shadow, not live. Some real information, small and fragile edge.

6Answers to Q1–Q16

✅ favourable or cleanly answered · ⚠️ mixed or unknown · ❌ against the edge

Q1❌Driven by a few weeks?
Yes. Top 5 of 86 events make ≈95% of PnL; without them +4% at 0 slippage and −12% at 0.5c. Only 28 of 86 events are net positive. Normal for long shots, but the edge's size can't be measured precisely.
Q2⚠️Realistic fills?
Partly. Mid + half the spread gives +61% at $10; real Jul–Oct fills were ≈4% worse (+54% vs +58%). The <2c band is effectively untradable (touch depth ≈$2). $50+ walks the book on ≈66% of trades.
Q3⚠️Is “too narrow” stable?
Only for weekly, and only early (1.25 SDs, 35% in the outer 20% at start; 1.03 and 25% pooled). From 85% elapsed the market is too wide. 48h is not too narrow (0.86, 13%). No stable direction.
Q4⚠️Where does it sit?
Upper-tail buckets (+151%, p=0.02 vs +23% lower tail; falls to +23% at 1c). 5–15c best (+81%); <2c dies at slippage. Leads 50–55% best, 90–95% lose.
Q5✅As-known count used?
Yes. Rebuilt with counts as known at the time; the look-ahead version differs by only $10–20.
Q6⚠️Independent bets?
86 events but only 44 non-overlapping weekly windows. Fri–Fri +59% (p=0.10), Tue–Tue +76% (p=0.05). 14-day block p: 0.018 / 0.09 / 0.28 at 0 / 0.5c / 1c.
Q7❌Who trades?
All 208 events, 179k wallets: top 50 hold 35% of volume and ≈1,100 market-maker-like wallets carry 36%. Crowd buyers of cheap upper-tail YES lost 95% of their stake; the model's selective upper-tail picks made +81% in the backtest. Pros sit on the other side.
Q8❌Repricing speed?
Fast, no latency edge. ≈88% of a burst is priced in before xtracker imports it. Buying after a burst returns −4%.
Q9❌Is the lag shrinking?
Yes, bots are here. Share priced in rose 0.60 → 0.80 → 0.93 across thirds of the sample; price/count correlation 0.13 → ≈0.5.
Q10⚠️Related markets
Inventory done: about 8 free tracker-settled weekly post-count families ($10–60k a week). Few good siblings; view-count markets have no free settlement data.
Q11❌Does it replicate on related markets?
No. 7 families, 388 events: +76% at 0 slippage (p=0.08), +1% at 0.5c, −18% at 1c. Model Brier worse than market in 5 of 7.
Q12❌Same market on other venues?
No live cross-check. Kalshi KXELONTWEETS dormant since Apr 2025; Manifold has none. Kalshi KXTRUTHSOCIAL could cross-check Trump.
Q13⚠️Tracker reliability
Normal lag 2–3 min. Late imports <1% Dec–Jul, 3.7% Aug–Sep, 26% in Oct 2026. Longest outages 57.7h and 23.5h. Treat live count as a lower bound. Backtest unaffected.
Q14⚠️Maker or taker?
Unknown: resting orders can't be backtested without book history. Maker fee 0 with 25% rebate; median reward $15/day per market. Passive bids on cheap tails face adverse selection. Shadow logger stores books.
Q15❌Capacity at 1c
≈8 trades a week, ≈$10/week PnL at 1c (≈$30 at 0.5c). Realistically $50–150/week.
Q16✅Resolution risk
6,125 of 6,132 markets resolved cleanly; 12 disputes, none changed the outcome; tracker matched winner in 210 of 212 events. Main risk: rising late imports.

8Go / no-go

GO-to-shadow, not live

3 biggest reasons for

  • Real information: model weight 0.93 vs price 0.10 when regressing outcomes; beats a no-information null at 0.5c.
  • +61% ROI (p=0.042) at realistic $10 fills.
  • Shadow logger is free and already built; settlement is clean.

3 biggest risks

  • Forking paths: p=0.45 after correcting for variants tried, and 1c slippage leaves +16% (p=0.24).
  • No replication on related markets, bots and pros are present, capacity only tens of dollars a week.
  • Tracker late imports hit 26% in Oct 2026; Sep and Oct were negative.

Shadow checklist (all must pass before any live money)

  1. At least 8 weeks and at least 15 resolved weekly events logged.
  2. Paper ROI at the logged ask of at least +15%, and still above 0 after dropping the top 2 events.
  3. Event-clustered p below 0.10, or below 0.20 if the ≥5c arm agrees in sign and size.
  4. Median ask-minus-mid gap on traded buckets ≤ 0.5c, and median $ within 2c of the ask ≥ $25.
  5. Tracker late-import share (>1 hour late) under 10%, and zero resolution mismatches.

The rule is frozen (same spec as the backtest; secondary arm is entries ≥5c only). If it passes: start live at $10 a trade, a $100 cap per event, taker only, buckets of 5c or more. Run the shadow logger for 8–12 weeks.
